Found the bug — it's not in the export, it's in your calibration correspondences.
3 of your 4 points share pitch x = 0 (i.e. they all sit on the left goal line):
pt	pixel -> pitch
1	(803, 135) -> (0, 0)
2	(730, 221) -> (0, 9.15)
3	(1183, 145) -> (9.15, 0)
4	(123, 1021) -> (0, 58.85)
getPerspectiveTransform needs to observe the along-pitch-length direction from at least two distinct pitch x values. With three corners pinned to x=0, the goal-line direction dominates and the matrix collapses — it predicts pitch x ≈ 0 for everything, including its own third input (which was supposed to map to (9.15, 0) but the fitted matrix maps it back to (0.000, 0.000)). Then export does raw - (52.5, 34), so every raw x≈0 becomes x≈-52.5 (the left goal line in centered coords), and _clip_to_pitch saturates y into [0, 68] → ±34 after centering. That's the "same position" you see for players 2/3/4: they're all pinned against the left goal line because the homography has no information about the length axis.
It is unbiased noise for player 1 only because player 1's box actually sits near the calibrated pixel region and on the goal line itself, where the homography is least wrong.
