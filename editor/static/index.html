    Pitch calibration

    Mark ≥4 pixel → pitch correspondences. Camera movement is referenced to frame 0, so points must be marked there — switch to frame 0 before placing them. Pitch coords are corner-origin meters (x: 0…length from the left goal line; y: 0…width from the top touchline). The exporter centers the origin afterward.

    Mapping points must span both pitch axes: use at least two distinct x values and at least two distinct y values. Piling all points on one goal-line (e.g. all x=0) leaves the pitch-length direction unobservable — every box then collapses to that line. Pick landmarks from across the visible pitch (corners, penalty spot, halfway line, …).

      Click on the frame to add a point.

      Ball trajectories

      Mark a frame range where the ball is in the air, plus the max height it reaches. The exporter fits a parabola: z = 0 at the two endpoints (kick & landing) and z = max height at the midpoint. Ranges must not overlap.
